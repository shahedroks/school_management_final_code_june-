## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I log in?

Garini accounts are usually created and managed by your school or institution. If you do not have login credentials, contact your school administrator or use the support email above.

### I forgot my password

Password reset is handled by your school or institution. Contact your administrator or email support with your registered email address.

### How do I submit an assignment?

1. Open the assignment from your student dashboard.
2. Tap to upload or attach your file.
3. Confirm the submission before the due date.

If upload fails, check your internet connection and ensure the file format is supported by your school.

### How do teachers create lessons or live sessions?

Teachers can manage lessons, assignments, and live sessions from the teacher dashboard after signing in with a teacher account.

### The app is not loading or shows an error

Try these steps:

1. Check your internet connection.
2. Close and reopen the app.
3. Update Garini to the latest version from the App Store or Google Play.
4. If the problem continues, contact support with the error message and your device model.

### How do I update the app?

Open the **App Store** (iOS) or **Google Play Store** (Android), search for **Garini**, and tap **Update** if a new version is available.
